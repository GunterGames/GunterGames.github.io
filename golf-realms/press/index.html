Short description

Real golf. Unreal places. Golf Realms is a free 3D golf game for iPhone that you swing with one finger. Play 20 full-size courses and 7 themed mini golf parks, from seaside cliffs and dinosaur islands to fairways above the clouds and low-gravity links on the Moon.

Long description

Golf Realms is golf you swing with one finger: swipe back, swipe through, and watch the ball fly, curve, bounce and roll with real physics. Read the wind, play the slope, shape your shot and drain the putt.

There are 360 holes across 20 full-size courses, from tree-lined parkland and seaside cliffs to glacial rivers and highlands under the northern lights. Then the realms go further: dinosaurs roaming island lagoons, an elven village in the giant redwoods, the ruins of Atlantis, cherry blossom and floating lanterns, airships over the Steamworks, a forest of giant glowing mushrooms, a desert of dragon bones under a ringed planet, a stormy coast where the kraken stirs, fairways floating above the clouds, lava fields, and the Moon with Earth overhead.

Seven themed mini golf parks add 126 more holes, from Candyland Commons and Pirate Cove to Haunted Hollow and a Glow Lagoon, played with the same ball and cup as the big courses.

Every day brings the Daily Cup, where everyone plays the same front nine in the same wind with one round each on a shared board. Players can also set up community tournaments with their own course, wind and entry fee, climb five tours, chase course records, and play friends online in rooms of up to 4 players.

Golf Realms is made by Gunter Games, an independent solo developer.

Features

  • 20 full-size courses, 360 holes, from parkland and seaside cliffs to Atlantis, Dino Isles and the Moon. Five are premium, unlocked with gems.
  • 7 themed mini golf parks, 126 holes: bank shots, bridges, tunnels, a windmill, island greens and two-way forks.
  • A skill-based one-finger swing: a straight, smooth swipe is pure; drift and you push, pull, hook or slice. Spin, draw, fade, punch and flop shots.
  • Real ball physics: wind, gusts, sloping lies and every surface change how the ball flies and rolls.
  • Read the green yourself with a slope grid and a short aim line.
  • Flyovers of every hole and instant replays.
  • The Daily Cup: one shared front nine a day, prizes for the top finishers and a seven-day streak bonus.
  • Community tournaments with player-set courses, wind, rounds and entry fees.
  • Play with friends online, up to 4 players, in stroke play, match play or skins.
  • Five tours with seasons against 29 rivals, plus the Daily Hole-in-One, daily challenges, 37 achievements and course medals.
  • Course records, handicap and strokes gained.
  • A style shop of outfits, balls, club finishes, trails and celebrations. From December 1, Frostpeak Summit becomes the North Pole for the holidays.
