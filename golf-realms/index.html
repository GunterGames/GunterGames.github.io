Skill swing

A swing that rewards skill

A straight, smooth swipe is pure; drift and you push, pull, hook or slice. Add backspin or topspin, draw or fade, punch or flop. Wind, gusts, sloping lies and every surface change how the ball flies and rolls.

Putting

Read the green yourself

Putts show a short aim line, and the slope grid shows how the green moves under your ball. The break is yours to judge.

Flyover

Fly the hole first

A flyover shows each hole before your first shot, and instant replays bring back the shots worth seeing again.

Compete every day

Daily Cup and tournaments

The Daily Cup

Every day, everyone plays the same front nine in the same wind, with one round each on a shared board. The top finishers win prizes when the day ends, and seven days in a row earns a streak bonus.

Community tournaments

Set up your own: pick the course, the wind, whether club upgrades count, the rounds, how long it runs and an entry fee in coins or gems. When time's up, the whole pool goes to the top finishers.

Play your way

Something new every day

Climb the tours

Five tours, from the Challenger Tour to the Realms Tour: seasons against 29 rivals, with the field, rounds per event and round length you choose. The top 10 in a season win season-end prizes.

Daily goals

  • The Daily Hole-in-One: a new par 3 every day
  • Three daily challenges and a daily check-in
  • A player level and 37 achievements

Course records

Play 9 or 18 holes in stroke play. Every 18-hole round can post to that course's leaderboard. Earn bronze, silver and gold medals, and track your handicap and strokes gained as you improve.
