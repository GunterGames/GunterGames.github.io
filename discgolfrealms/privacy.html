Disc Golf Realms Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Developer: Gunter Games. Contact: guntergamesdev@gmail.com

Disc Golf Realms is a disc golf game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. There are no Disc Golf Realms accounts: you never give us your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your progress

Your progress, coins, gems, discs, looks, settings and best scores are saved on your iPhone. We can't see them.

So that deleting and reinstalling the game, or moving to a new phone, doesn't lose them, a copy is kept in your iCloud (iCloud key-value storage), if you're signed in to iCloud. It's stored by Apple in your iCloud, not by us, and we can't see it. When the game is installed again, it offers to restore the copy. You can turn off iCloud for Disc Golf Realms in the iPhone's Settings › your name › iCloud.

Player ID and name

To post scores, the game uses a player ID: if you're signed in to Game Center, one made on your iPhone from the player ID Game Center gives our games (so your scores follow you to your other devices); otherwise a random ID created on your iPhone and kept in its Keychain (so reinstalling the game keeps it). Neither is your Apple Account, your name or your email address. A secret key, also kept on your iPhone, proves the ID is yours when you rename yourself or delete your scores.

Your name is the one you type in the game, or your Game Center nickname if you're signed in and haven't typed one, or "Player" and four characters. Names are checked automatically, and names with slurs or obscenities aren't accepted.

Leaderboards, the daily hole and challenges

When you finish an 18-hole round, it's posted to that course's leaderboard so other players can see it: your player ID, your name, the course, your score on each hole and the date. Your first finish of the day's daily hole is posted the same way. A challenge you send stores the course, the hole, your score, your player ID and name, and the scores of the friends who play it with their IDs and names.

These are stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, until you delete them or we remove them.

Deleting your scores: Settings › Account & privacy › Delete my online scores removes every round, daily score and challenge you've posted. You can also ask us at guntergamesdev@gmail.com.

Reporting and hiding players

You can report a player on a leaderboard for an offensive name or an impossible score, or hide them. A report stores the player's ID, the reason and your player ID. A player reported by several people is hidden from the leaderboards until we review it. Reports aren't visible to other players. Hidden players are kept on your iPhone only.

Online games with friends

When you play online with a room code, your player ID, your name, your thrower's look, your disc and your throws are sent to the other players in the room through Supabase's real-time service while the game is on. We don't keep a record of online games afterwards.

Game Center

Signing in to Game Center is optional. If you're signed in, Disc Golf Realms uses your Game Center nickname and player ID as described above. Apple's Game Center is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Disc Golf Realms offers optional purchases (Remove Ads and gem packs). Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The game checks with Apple which purchases you own.

Ads

Disc Golf Realms is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional, such as watching an ad for coins, gems or a mulligan. Others appear between holes, at most every few holes. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking,
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location,
  • information about the ads you see and tap,
  • app performance and crash information.

Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Disc Golf Realms may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads aren't personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in the iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): you're asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your answer in Settings › Account & privacy › Ad privacy choices.
  • Remove Ads turns off the ads between holes.
  • Ads in Disc Golf Realms are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

No analytics

Disc Golf Realms doesn't send gameplay statistics or analytics of its own.

Children

Disc Golf Realms isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has posted a score or name, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
