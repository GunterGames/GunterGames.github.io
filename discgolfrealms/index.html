Common questions

How do I throw? Below the dashed line, swipe down to reach back (further down means more power), then straight up to release. Above the line, drag to aim: sideways turns your line, up and down sets the launch angle. Settings › How to play has the full guide.

Why does my disc curve? Every disc flies by its four numbers. Turn makes a fast disc drift right (for a right-handed backhand) early in its flight; fade pulls it left at the end. Throwing with hyzer (outside edge down) curves it further left, anhyzer to the right. A forehand curves the other way.

How do I putt? Inside 79 ft you putt. Stop your swipe on the gold mark to reach the chains on flat ground. Too hard, or off the edge, and the disc can chain out.

How do I open more courses? Play nine holes of a course to open the next one.

What are plastics? Sticky plastic lands and stays; skippy plastic skips and slides on. Earn them by throwing a disc, or buy them with coins, in the Bag.

How do I play with friends? Online › Host a round, then share the room's code. Your friends tap Online › Join a friend and enter it. Up to four players throw the same hole at the same time.

I reinstalled the game. Where's my progress? If you were signed in to iCloud, the game offers to restore your progress when it first opens. With Game Center, your leaderboard scores are yours on every device.

How do I change my name or delete my scores? Settings › Gameplay › Your name. Settings › Account & privacy › Delete my online scores removes your scores from the leaderboards.

Someone on a leaderboard has an offensive name. Tap their row on the leaderboard, then Report. You can also hide them.
